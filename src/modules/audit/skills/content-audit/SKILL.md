# Content audit

Audit configured content roots; read system rules only as the evaluation basis.
Check filing, metadata, naming, links, orphans, duplication, fragmentation,
knowledge quality, contact structure, journal integration, lifecycle state,
contradictions, possible obsolescence, and missing evidence.

Use concrete files and evidence. Separate verified contradictions from tensions
or research suggestions. Do not infer staleness from dates alone. Classify and
prioritize each finding by practical effect, assign stable finding IDs, and
report strengths as well as problems. Do not change content; selected findings
move to the content-correction skill.
