# OS audit criteria

Evaluate:

1. layer and ownership separation;
2. completeness and consistency of canonical rules;
3. schema and register contracts;
4. workflow compatibility with those contracts;
5. templates, prompts, and skills applying rather than redefining rules;
6. runtime summaries not competing with canonical sources;
7. documentation, path, link, and naming quality;
8. operational maturity and deterministic validation;
9. active migration remnants or obsolete terminology.

Prioritize a finding when it crosses layers, misdirects users or agents, creates
repeatable defects, or materially increases maintenance cost. Ignore cosmetic
preference without operational impact.
