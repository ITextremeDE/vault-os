# Journal

The journal records time-bound context, observation, reflection, and periodic
distillation. It is not the permanent knowledge store, a project substitute,
or an archive.

Use the configured daily, weekly, and yearly filename rules exactly. Create a
weekly note only when several days reveal a real development, decision, or
insight. Move durable outcomes to their natural project, area, resource, or
knowledge note. Journal files remain in their time structure and are not moved
to the general archive.
