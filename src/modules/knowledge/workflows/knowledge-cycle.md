# Knowledge cycle

## Capture

Read the source, search for natural target notes, compare new material with the
current corpus, and distinguish confirmation, extension, contradiction, and
possible obsolescence. Keep the source distinct from derived claims. Filing or
indexing alone is not integration.

## Use

Read relevant originals, separate evidence from inference, answer from reliable
sources, and return only durable, reusable results to a canonical note within
the authorized scope. Not every answer belongs in the vault.

## Maintain

Look for contradictions, stale claims, missing evidence, isolated clusters,
fragmentation, missing links, and concepts without a natural home. Audits produce
findings first; external research, content changes, and deletion require explicit
scope. Preserve manual content and uncertainty.
