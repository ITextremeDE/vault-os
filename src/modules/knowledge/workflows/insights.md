# Insights

An insight is a focused, durable claim that remains understandable beyond its
origin. It is not raw conversation, unresolved exploration, source material, or
a project update.

Prefer one supporting claim per note. Record the statement, reasoning, origin,
and relevant links. Refine an existing insight before creating a variant. Use a
working note until the claim is stable enough to stand on its own.
