# Audit trigger rules

Prefer robust patterns over sensitive thresholds and trends over isolated events.

- One local documentation gap or up to two isolated notes: observe.
- Repeated metadata, naming, link, or filing errors: content audit.
- Repeated drift between canonical rules, assets, or runtime: OS audit.
- Growing inbox, isolated clusters, or fragmented knowledge: content audit.
- Several categories, multiple layers, or unclear cause: full vault audit.
- A worsening trend raises the recommendation by one level.

No action is valid when signals are isolated and have no meaningful consequence.
Continuous audit is valid for light drift that needs observation rather than a
deep pass. Direct correction remains a narrow exception.
