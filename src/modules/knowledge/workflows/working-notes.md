# Working notes

Working notes are the processing space between source material and durable
knowledge. Use them to compare, structure, analyze, or distill conversations,
resources, and ongoing work. They may remain exploratory or contradictory.

Name the subject being processed, not merely its source. Record the starting
point, core ideas, synthesis, possible insights, and open questions. Update an
existing working note when it is the natural home.
