# Knowledge notes

Knowledge notes preserve reusable understanding such as guides, models,
patterns, recipes, or overviews. Use resources for material and references,
journal notes for chronology, projects for outcomes, and insights for a single
durable claim.

A good knowledge note has a clear title, a distilled core, enough explanation
to remain useful, relevant sources, and links to related notes. Prefer extending
the canonical note over creating parallel variants.
